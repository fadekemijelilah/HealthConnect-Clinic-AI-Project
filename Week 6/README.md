# HealthConnect – Week 6

## Integration, Advanced Development & Validation

Week 6 focused on improving, integrating, validating, and documenting the outputs developed during Week 5 in preparation for Week 7 testing.

### Project Objective

HealthConnect aims to reduce missed appointments, improve attendance, optimise appointment slot utilisation, and strengthen administrative support using data and AI.

## Week 6 Focus

- Workstream integration
- Advanced data analysis and KPI validation
- Model improvement and validation
- ML pipeline integration
- Generative AI assistant evaluation and refinement
- Risk and dependency management
- Cross-track coordination
- Week 7 testing preparation

## Workstream Progress

### Data Analytics

Conducted deeper analysis and KPI validation covering booking lead time, previous no-show history, reminder status/channel, appointment type, distance, and patient segments. Dashboard outputs and evidence-based operational recommendations were refined.

### Data Science

Conducted formal error analysis, resolved feature redundancy, and tuned a Gradient Boosting candidate. Model performance improved from 62.4% to 64.8% accuracy and from 0.677 to 0.687 ROC-AUC.

### ML Engineering

Integrated preprocessing, feature engineering, and the candidate model into a reusable pipeline. Schema validation, batch prediction, configurable thresholds, save/reload functionality, and integration testing were completed, with 10/10 tests passed.

### Generative AI

Focused on evaluating and refining the HealthConnect assistant. Progress, issues, dependencies, and integration outcomes are being documented as the workstream is finalised.

### Project Management

Coordinated cross-track integration, dependencies, risks, issues, communication, timeline updates, project status tracking, and Week 7 testing preparation.

## Cross-Track Integration

Key integration activities included:

- Data Analytics ↔ Data Science
- Data Science → ML Engineering
- ML Engineering → downstream consumers
- Data Analytics → Project Management
- Project Management → all workstreams
- Generative AI → Project Management / cross-track integration

## Key Week 6 Outcome

The project progressed from separate Week 5 deliverables toward integrated and validated workstreams. ML Engineering successfully validated the integrated pipeline with 10/10 tests passed, while Data Science and Data Analytics continued model and evidence refinement.

## Week 7

The next phase will focus on:

**Testing → Refinement → End-to-End Validation**

Testing will cover Analytics, Data Science, ML Engineering, Generative AI, and cross-track end-to-end validation.
